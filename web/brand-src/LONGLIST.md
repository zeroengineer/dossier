# Dossier — research and concept longlist

## Category research (library + own knowledge)
- `search_library.py --industry developer-tools --summary`: abstract 25 %, pictorial 22 %, combination 22 %,
  letterform 11 %; multi-colour and blue dominate; containment (34 %) and negative space (19 %) are the common techniques.
- `--subject document`: AWS CloudFormation (document template), DocumentDB (cylinder + braces), Docusaurus (mascot
  holding a page). Paper: paper planes (Telegram, lighttpd, Mailjet), folded facets (Origami). Folder: Brandfolder.
- Clichés to avoid: folder, filing cabinet, sheet with folded corner and lines, magnifying glass, cloud, shield/lock,
  paper plane, paper clip, check mark, blue/teal.

## Word map (abridged)
- dossier → a bound file on one subject, jacket, spine, index tab, string-and-button closure, stamp, record number
- precise → grid, right angle, one exact cut, registration/crop marks
- quiet → negative space, restraint, one accent, closed shapes
- trustworthy → bound, sealed, stable base, closed loop
- modern → geometric primitives, rounded-rect geometry matching the UI radius
- Intersections: **D + spine (binding)**, **stack + closure button (sealed)**, **jacket + index tab (D in negative)**

## Longlist (one line each; score = clarity / distinction / simplicity / relevance / 16 px, 1–5 each)
1. **Bound D** (letterform): a D split into a spine bar and a bowl by one precise gap; the spine is the red binding. 5/4/5/5/5 = 24
2. **Sealed stack** (abstract): two offset slabs (flap and body) held shut by a round closure button in red. 4/4/4/5/4 = 21
3. **Tabbed monogram** (negative-space lettermark): a solid jacket with a D cut out of it and one index tab on its edge in red. 4/4/4/5/4 = 21
4. Crop-mark D: a D implied only by four printer's crop marks. 3/4/3/3/1 = 14
5. Ledger D: a D built from horizontal rows (records in order). 3/2/3/4/2 = 14 (reads as document-with-lines)
6. Ribbon tie: a square crossed by a red band, a dossier tied shut. 3/3/5/4/4 = 19
7. Registry notch: a circle with one exact notch at 2 o'clock marked red. 2/2/5/2/5 = 16
8. Two-page D: a D with an offset outline behind it (a second version). 3/2/3/4/2 = 14
9. Spine and dividers: a tall bar with three short bars stepping off it (index divider tabs). 3/3/3/4/3 = 16
10. Stamp D: a D inside a rounded square with a chamfered corner like a rubber stamp. 3/2/4/3/4 = 16

**Built:** 1 (letterform), 2 (abstract), 3 (negative-space monogram in a container): the three strongest and
most different mark types.
